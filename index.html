About me

Hi, I'm Bill Gates.

I'm a technology entrepreneur, software developer, and philanthropist. I co-founded Microsoft in 1975 with the vision of making computers accessible to everyone. Over the years, I've been involved in developing software that helped shape the personal computing revolution.

Today, much of my work focuses on global health, education, clean energy, and reducing poverty through the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation. I'm passionate about innovation, problem-solving, and finding ways technology can improve people's lives.

When I'm not working on philanthropic initiatives, I enjoy reading, learning about science, and exploring new ideas that can help solve some of the world's biggest challenges.
